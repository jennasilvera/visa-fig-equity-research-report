# Trading Comparable Company Analysis

## Purpose

Trading comps benchmark Visa against publicly traded payments companies. This is one of the most important valuation methods for equity research because it reflects how the market currently values similar business models.

## Peer Group Construction

A professional comps analysis should not use one undifferentiated peer set. Visa should be compared across multiple groups.

## Peer Groups

### Global Networks

| Company | Relevance |
|---|---|
| Mastercard | Closest public peer |
| American Express | Closed-loop network and payments competitor |
| Discover / Capital One | Domestic card network / issuer context |

### Merchant Acquirers and Processors

| Company | Relevance |
|---|---|
| Fiserv | Merchant acquiring and processing |
| Global Payments | Merchant acquiring and issuer processing |
| Shift4 | Higher-growth acquiring / verticalized payments |
| Adyen | Global acquiring / PSP benchmark |
| Toast | Vertical SaaS and payments reference |

### Digital Wallets and Fintech Platforms

| Company | Relevance |
|---|---|
| PayPal | Wallet and checkout competitor |
| Block | Consumer and merchant fintech platform |

### Infrastructure and Issuer Processing

| Company | Relevance |
|---|---|
| FIS | Payments and financial technology infrastructure |
| Jack Henry | Bank technology / processing reference |
| Marqeta | Issuer processing and modern card issuing reference |

## Metrics to Include

| Metric | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Market Capitalization | Size and scale |
| Enterprise Value | Capital-structure-neutral valuation |
| Revenue Growth | Growth profile |
| EBITDA Margin | Profitability and scalability |
| Operating Margin | Core operating efficiency |
| P/E | Primary large-cap equity research valuation metric |
| EV/EBITDA | Common public company valuation metric |
| FCF Yield | Cash generation relative to market value |
| PEG Ratio | Valuation relative to growth |
| Net Cash / Debt | Balance sheet context |

## Professional Interpretation

Visa should trade at a premium to most processors because it has:

- Higher margins
- Stronger network effects
- Lower balance-sheet risk
- Better free cash flow conversion
- Greater global acceptance
- Stronger brand and issuer relationships

Visa should be compared most carefully against Mastercard because Mastercard is the closest business-model peer.

## Required Output

The final model should produce:

1. Visa implied value using peer median P/E
2. Visa implied value using peer median EV/EBITDA
3. Visa premium / discount to Mastercard
4. Visa premium / discount to processor peers
5. Growth-adjusted valuation discussion
6. FCF yield comparison

## Interview Talking Point

> I separated valuation peers from strategic competitors. Mastercard is the closest valuation peer. Processors like Fiserv and Global Payments are useful context, but they have different margins and business models. Wallets and A2A rails are more strategic threats than direct valuation comps.
