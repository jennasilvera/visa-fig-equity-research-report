# Valuation Football Field

## Purpose

The football-field valuation summarizes Visa's implied equity value per share across multiple valuation methodologies.

## Methodologies to Include

| Method | Output |
|---|---|
| DCF - Perpetuity Growth | Implied share price range |
| DCF - Exit Multiple | Implied share price range |
| Forward P/E | Implied share price range |
| EV/EBITDA | Implied share price range |
| FCF Yield | Implied share price range |
| Historical P/E Range | Implied share price range |
| SOTP | Implied share price range |

## Final Output

The final report should include a football-field chart showing the valuation range and selected target price.
