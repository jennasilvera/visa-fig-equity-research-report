# Sum-of-the-Parts Valuation Framework

## Purpose

A SOTP framework helps separate Visa's core network economics from faster-growing value-added services and money movement opportunities.

## Illustrative Segments

| Segment | Valuation Basis | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Core Consumer Payments Network | EBIT or revenue multiple | Durable global network cash cow |
| Commercial and B2B Payments | Revenue or EBIT multiple | Long-term digitization opportunity |
| Value-Added Services | Revenue or EBIT multiple | Faster-growth risk, data, fraud, consulting, issuing, and acceptance services |
| Visa Direct / Money Movement | Revenue multiple | Non-carded money movement optionality |
| Net Cash / Other | Book value | Bridge to equity value |

## Key Questions

- Does Visa deserve one blended multiple or separate segment multiples?
- How much of Visa's valuation is supported by core network economics?
- How much option value should investors assign to VAS and Visa Direct?
- Is the market underestimating the services mix shift?
