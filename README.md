# Visa FIG Equity Research Report

This repository contains a professional-style equity research report on Visa Inc. (NYSE: V), built as a portfolio project for investment banking, equity research, FIG, and payments-sector recruiting.

## Project Objective

The goal is to demonstrate the ability to analyze a financial institutions / payments company using institutional research methods, including business model analysis, industry mapping, financial analysis, valuation, KPI tracking, risk assessment, earnings-call interpretation, and professional presentation.

## Contents

- `report/`: Full research report in PDF and Markdown.
- `model/`: Supporting Excel valuation and operating model.
- `data/`: Source documents, processed financial data, peer comps, and research notes.
- `notebooks/`: Optional analysis scripts or notebooks used to clean and visualize data.
- `presentation/`: Optional investment committee summary.

## Key Outputs

- Investment recommendation and 12-month target price.
- Historical financial analysis.
- Revenue stream and geographic mix analysis.
- Peer comparable company analysis.
- Bull/base/bear scenario valuation.
- KPI framework for Visa and payments networks.
- Earnings call analysis and recruiting Q&A.

## Suggested Workflow

1. Read the report first to understand the thesis and valuation conclusion.
2. Open the Excel model and review assumptions in the Forecast and Valuation sheets.
3. Update the peer comps sheet with fresh market data from FactSet, Capital IQ, Bloomberg, Koyfin, or company filings.
4. Expand the model with consensus estimates and a DCF if desired.
5. Use the Interview Prep sheet to prepare for FIG, IB, and equity research conversations.
