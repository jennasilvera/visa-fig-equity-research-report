# Visa KPI Dashboard Framework

## Purpose

The KPI dashboard should provide a one-screen summary of Visa's operating performance, valuation, and investment debates.

A professional dashboard should help a recruiter, analyst, or investor quickly understand what drives Visa's revenue, margins, EPS, valuation, and risk/reward.

---

## Dashboard Sections

| Section | Metrics |
|---|---|
| Recommendation Summary | Recommendation, target price, implied upside, primary valuation method |
| Revenue Growth | Net revenue, revenue growth, revenue CAGR |
| Network KPIs | Payments volume, processed transactions, credentials, acceptance locations |
| Cross-Border | Cross-border volume, international transaction revenue, cross-border yield |
| Monetization | Gross revenue yield, net revenue yield, client incentives / gross revenue |
| Value-Added Services | VAS revenue, VAS growth, VAS as % of revenue |
| Profitability | Operating income, operating margin, free cash flow margin |
| Capital Allocation | Buybacks, dividends, diluted share count, EPS accretion |
| Valuation | P/E, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield, DCF value, scenario target prices |
| Risk | Regulatory heatmap, A2A risk, stablecoin risk, client incentive risk |

---

## Required Charts

1. Revenue and operating margin trend
2. Payments volume and processed transaction trend
3. Cross-border volume trend
4. Client incentives as % of gross revenue
5. VAS growth and mix
6. Visa vs Mastercard relative growth
7. Peer P/E and EV/EBITDA comparison
8. DCF sensitivity
9. Bull/base/bear scenario output
10. Football-field valuation range

Investors use dashboards to identify quickly:

- What changed?
- What matters?
- What is driving the stock?
- What is the market debating?
- What is the downside?
