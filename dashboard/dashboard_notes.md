# KPI Dashboard Notes

## Purpose

The KPI dashboard should summarize Visa's operating performance, valuation, and investment debates in a recruiter-friendly format.

## Dashboard Sections

1. Recommendation summary
2. Revenue and EPS trend
3. Payments volume and processed transaction trend
4. Cross-border volume trend
5. Client incentives as percentage of gross revenue
6. VAS growth and revenue mix
7. Operating margin trend
8. Free cash flow and buybacks
9. Peer valuation comparison
10. Scenario valuation output

## Required Charts

- Revenue growth
- Operating margin
- Payments volume
- Processed transactions
- Cross-border volume
- Client incentives
- VAS mix
- P/E peer comparison
- EV/EBITDA peer comparison
- Bull/base/bear target price
