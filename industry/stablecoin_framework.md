# Stablecoin Framework

## Purpose

Stablecoins are both a potential threat and a potential opportunity for Visa. A serious payments-sector analysis should not treat stablecoins as either irrelevant hype or guaranteed disruption.

The key question is:

> Do stablecoins bypass Visa, or does Visa become part of the trusted infrastructure that connects stablecoins to consumers, merchants, banks, and enterprises?

## Stablecoin Threat Case

Stablecoins could pressure Visa if they become widely used for cross-border settlement, remittances, B2B payments, marketplace payouts, treasury movement, merchant settlement, wallet-to-wallet transfers, and emerging-market dollarized payments.

## Stablecoin Opportunity Case

Visa may participate in stablecoin growth through settlement infrastructure, on/off-ramp connectivity, tokenized credentials, compliance and risk tools, merchant acceptance, cross-border money movement, and bank / fintech partnerships.

## Where Stablecoins Are Most Relevant

| Use Case | Relevance | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-border B2B settlement | High | Current systems can be slow and expensive |
| Remittances | Medium/High | Cost and speed matter |
| Marketplace payouts | Medium | Faster payout capability |
| Treasury movement | Medium | Enterprise settlement use case |
| Consumer checkout | Low/Medium | UX, rewards, disputes, and regulation remain barriers |
| Domestic credit spend | Low | Stablecoins do not replicate credit or rewards |

## Impact on Visa's Model

| Model Area | Potential Impact |
|---|---|
| Cross-Border Revenue | Stablecoins could pressure traditional cross-border settlement economics |
| Visa Direct | Stablecoins could compete with or complement money movement |
| VAS Revenue | Compliance, fraud, identity, and risk tools could benefit |
| Take Rate | Lower-cost rails could pressure monetization |
| Terminal Multiple | Investors may assign lower or higher multiple depending on Visa's role |

## Investment Conclusion

Stablecoins are not an immediate existential threat to Visa's core consumer card business. They are more relevant to cross-border settlement, B2B flows, remittances, and payout infrastructure.
