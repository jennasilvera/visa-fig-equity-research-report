# Source Notes for Investment Memo

## Primary Public Sources to Use

- Visa FY2025 Annual Report
- Visa FY2026 quarterly earnings releases
- Visa investor relations quarterly earnings materials
- Visa earnings call transcripts
- Mastercard annual report and investor materials
- Peer company annual reports
- Public regulatory sources
- Public market data

## Current Source-Backed Facts to Verify Before Final Publication

- FY2025 net revenue
- FY2025 payments volume
- FY2025 processed transactions
- FY2025 credentials
- Q2 FY2026 net revenue growth
- Q2 FY2026 payments volume growth
- Q2 FY2026 processed transaction growth
- Q2 FY2026 VAS growth
- Share repurchase authorization
- Current share price
- Current market capitalization
- Current peer multiples

## Citation Standard

Every final report, memo, and model output should cite:

1. Source document
2. Publication date
3. Relevant page, section, or filing reference
4. Calculation methodology, if the figure is calculated
