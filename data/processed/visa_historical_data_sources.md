# Visa Historical Data Sources

## Purpose

This file documents the source basis for the historical financials and operating KPIs used in the institutional Visa model.

## Primary Sources

- Visa FY2025 Annual Report
- Visa FY2025 Form 10-K
- Visa FY2024 Annual Report / Form 10-K
- Visa FY2022 Form 10-K
- Visa FY2021 Annual Report

## Financial Statement Items

The following items are sourced from Visa annual reports and 10-K filings:

- Net revenue
- Service revenue
- Data processing revenue
- International transaction revenue
- Other revenue
- Client incentives
- Operating expenses
- Operating income
- Operating margin
- Net income
- Diluted EPS
- Operating cash flow
- Capital expenditures
- Free cash flow

## Operating KPIs

The following operating KPIs are sourced from Visa annual report operational highlights:

- Total volume
- Payments volume
- Processed transactions
- Payment credentials

## Notes

- Dollar figures in the historical financials CSV are in millions, except EPS.
- Payments volume and total volume are shown in trillions.
- Processed transactions are shown in billions.
- Payment credentials are shown in billions.
- Free cash flow is calculated as operating cash flow less capital expenditures.
- Net revenue yield is calculated as net revenue divided by payments volume.
- Processed transaction revenue per transaction is calculated as data processing revenue divided by processed transactions.
- VAS revenue is populated only where disclosed clearly in the available source set.
